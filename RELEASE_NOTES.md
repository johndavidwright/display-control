# DisplayControl 0.3.0

Update DisplayControl directly from the app with the new **Updates** section.
Check for a new version whenever you like, or turn on automatic checks and
optional automatic download and installation. Update downloads and the update
feed are signed and verified using Sparkle.

This release also includes the improvements from 0.2.5:

- Fixed the flash near the title when collapsing Color or Presets.
- Corrected RGB readings on the Corsair XENEON EDGE, addressing the startup
  “Could not confirm Blue” warning caused by its different color scale.
- Improved confirmation of monitor adjustments, saved settings, and recovery
  after reconnecting a display.

## Install

Requires **Apple Silicon** and **macOS 13 Ventura or later**.
Download **DisplayControl-0.3.0-macOS-arm64.zip** below, unzip it, and move
**DisplayControl.app** to **Applications**. Quit the older copy first.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not notarized by Apple**. macOS may require
approval on first launch; see the
[installation instructions](https://github.com/johndavidwright/display-control#install).

**Upgrading from 0.2.5 or earlier?** Install this version manually once.
Future releases can be installed from **Updates → Check for Updates…**.
